A bit about me

One perspective.
Many ways to make it real.

My practice moves between 3D trade-show booths, scenography, environmental graphics, brand identity, packaging, websites and campaigns. I also do white-label design for agencies. Across each format, I look for the idea that makes the work clear, distinctive, and useful.

Working abroad shaped how I make, communicate, and deliver design. Based in Paraguay, I keep the collaboration direct and adaptable for teams wherever they are.

I was Lead Designer at Tenth Element in Las Vegas, NV. I have worked as a remote freelance designer since 2018.
