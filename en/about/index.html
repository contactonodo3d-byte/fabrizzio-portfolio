I’m a graphic and exhibit designer in Asunción, Paraguay. I design 3D trade show booths, scenography and environmental graphics, brand identities, packaging, websites and campaigns. I also do white-label design for agencies, so a studio can present the work under its own name.

I was Lead Designer at Tenth Element in Las Vegas, Nevada, from 2020 to 2022, on graphic and web design. That period set the standard I still work to: clear communication, careful files, and delivery a US team can present. I’ve been a remote freelancer since 2018, with more than eight years of design experience. From Asunción, my workday overlaps US time zones, and I work with brands, agencies and teams abroad.

My booth work covers the job from concept to a production handoff: layout, 3D visualization, graphics, and the files a fabricator needs. I don’t build or install the stand; I coordinate the design with the builder you name. When a review needs it, I provide a 3D/AR model with a NODO3D viewer. El Delta and Foody · ExpoVet are built projects, shown as renders next to photographs of the finished space. Gestecner · Navegestic is a documented concept, with technical views and an interactive model. Indufar Run is a set of five booths for six brands.

I work in Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Blender and DaVinci Resolve. Engagement can be hourly at $35, a monthly retainer, or a fixed project fee.
