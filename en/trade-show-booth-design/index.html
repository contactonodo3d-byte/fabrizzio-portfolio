Trade show booth design for exhibit builders.

I’m Fabrizzio Ruiz, a freelance trade show booth designer and exhibit design freelancer based in Asunción, Paraguay. I design the concept, the 3D visualization and the graphics, then hand you production-ready files your fabricators can build from.

I don’t fabricate or install the booth. I work with the builder you already use, or the one your client has chosen, and I coordinate the design details with that team. I’ve been a remote freelancer since 2018, with more than eight years of experience, and I was Lead Designer for graphic and web work at Tenth Element in Las Vegas, Nevada, from 2020 to 2022. I work hours that overlap US time zones. I can work with exhibit builders in the United States and Canada, including teams in Las Vegas, Chicago or Orlando.

Services

Concepts and layout.

I plan product placement, circulation, visitor reception and activations inside the footprint you have. The concept has to work as a space, not only as a picture.

3D renders and models.

I build renders, views and models so your team, and your client, can review the design before anyone cuts material. When a review needs to go further, I can provide a 3D/AR viewer (NODO3D). The Gestecner Navegestic concept, for example, can be rotated on the desktop and opened in augmented reality on a compatible phone.

Graphics and content.

I design brand applications for walls, headers, counters and other surfaces, and screen content when the project includes it, from stills to motion.

Production-ready files.

After approval, I prepare the graphic and documentation package we agreed in the proposal, and I coordinate remaining design details with the stand builder.

Deliverables

The exact package is written into the proposal before I start. Depending on the job, it can include a concept presentation; 3D renders of the approved design; alternative concepts, when the brief calls for more than one option; dimensioned views, floor plans and technical views; a 3D model and, when useful, an AR review; production graphic files for walls, headers, furniture and other branded surfaces; and screen content or motion, when the booth includes displays.

I confirm formats and scope before we begin. If your shop has a drawing standard or a preferred graphic spec, send it with the brief.

How I work

  1. Event and scope. We review the date, dimensions, budget and fabricator requirements. Tell me who will build the space and what documentation they need.
  2. Design and review. I develop the proposal and refine it with your team. You can use the renders and, when we include it, the 3D/AR model in your client presentation.
  3. Production handoff. I prepare the agreed deliverables and coordinate design details with the stand builder.

FAQ

Can I hire you if I already have a fabricator?

Yes. Tell me who will produce the space and what documentation they need. We define the design and the files around that process.

What do I receive at the end?

The deliverables are agreed in the proposal. They can include renders, a concept presentation, dimensioned views and graphic files for production. We confirm formats and scope before we begin.

Does the service include fabrication and installation?

No. This is a design and visualization service. I coordinate the design with the builder; I don’t build or install the booth myself. If you also need production or installation, say so when you ask for a quote.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on the dimensions, complexity, deliverables and the event date. Share those details and I’ll send a proposal. My hourly rate is $35. Project work is scoped as a fixed fee.

Do you work with exhibit builders in the United States and Canada?

Yes. I work freelance from Asunción, with hours that overlap US time zones, and I can work with builders in Las Vegas, Chicago, Orlando and other US or Canadian markets.

Can you show a built booth next to the 3D render?

Yes, on El Delta and on Foody · ExpoVet. Indufar Run is published as design renders. Gestecner is a concept and has not been built.

Do you provide 3D and AR previews for a sales presentation?

Yes, when the scope includes them. The Gestecner concept is at https://fabrizzioruiz.com/en/expo/gestecner/.

What do you need to start?

The exhibition date, the booth dimensions and height limit, what needs to be shown, the brand guidelines, and the fabricator’s requirements when you have them. If you are bidding, tell me the submission date.
