White-label design for US and Canadian agencies.

I’m Fabrizzio Ruiz, a freelance white label designer for agencies. I’m a senior graphic, web and 3D designer based in Asunción, Paraguay. I work under your brand, and your client does not have to know I’m there.

I was Lead Designer for graphic and web work at Tenth Element in Las Vegas, Nevada, from 2020 to 2022. I’ve been a remote freelancer since 2018, with more than eight years of experience. I use Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Blender and DaVinci Resolve, and I can provide a 3D/AR review with a NODO3D viewer when a project needs it.

What I do under your brand

You stay the point of contact. I design, revise and deliver the files, and you present the work as yours. That can include trade show booth and exhibit design (concepts, 3D renders, graphics and production-ready files); branding and packaging; campaign and presentation design; web and digital design; and 3D/AR reviews so your team or your client can walk a concept before it is built.

I work from your guidelines, templates, file naming and review process. If you need me in your Slack, Figma or Adobe files, I work there. The finished files are yours to deliver.

I contributed to the projects in my portfolio in different ways. I led the full scope on some and handled specific stages on others. Many were completed through agencies. On El Delta and Gestecner, the design and 3D visualization are mine, and the work was carried out through Péscalo.

NDA and confidentiality

White-label work only holds if your client relationship stays protected. I don’t contact your client, I don’t present the work under my name, and I don’t share project files outside the people you name.

Hours and time zone overlap

I’m based in Asunción, Paraguay (UTC−3). That puts me one to two hours ahead of US Eastern Time and four to five hours ahead of US Pacific Time, so my workday already overlaps US business hours.

Turnaround

Scope, the event date and the number of revision rounds always affect the schedule. I confirm the timeline in the proposal before we start.

Engagement models

We can work in three ways. I’ll recommend one after I see the work.

Hourly — $35/hour.

Best when the scope is still moving. I track hours and check in before I go past an agreed cap.

Retainer.

A set monthly capacity for agencies with recurring work. The format, capacity and monthly scope are defined together before we begin.

Per project.

A fixed fee for a defined deliverable, such as a booth concept set, a production file package, a campaign, or a website design. Scope, revisions, timeline and fee go into the proposal before work starts.

How I work

  1. Brief and fit. You send the client context, the guidelines, the deadline and, for a booth, the footprint and fabricator requirements. We agree on scope, fee or hourly cap, and the NDA.
  2. Design and review. I develop the work and review it with your team, in your tools, under your name. You run the client presentation.
  3. Delivery. I hand over the agreed files, packaged the way your team needs them. On exhibit work, I also coordinate design details with the builder you name.

FAQ

What does it mean that you are a white label graphic designer for agencies?

I design the work and you deliver it under your brand. I follow your process, stay off the client channel unless you ask otherwise, and I don’t market the project as mine without written permission.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes.

Do you work with agencies in the United States and Canada?

Yes. I’m a remote graphic designer for US agencies and for teams in Canada, based in Asunción, Paraguay, with hours that overlap US time zones.

What tools do you use?

Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Blender and DaVinci Resolve. For booth walkthroughs, I can provide a 3D/AR review with a NODO3D viewer, as I did for the Gestecner concept.

Do you only do brand and web work, or also exhibits?

Both. White-label work can be branding, packaging, campaigns, presentations, web design, or trade show booth and scenography design.

How do we start?

Email hello@fabrizzioruiz.com with the type of work, the deadline and, if you have it, the brief. I’ll come back with a recommended engagement model and a scope. WhatsApp: +595 972 780 989.

What do you need from us to work under your brand?

Your guidelines or the client’s, any templates or file-naming rules, the decision-maker on your team, and a clear deadline. For booth work, I also need the footprint, height limits, the event date and the fabricator’s requirements when you have them.

Can you handle ongoing work, not just a single project?

Yes. The monthly design partnership is for recurring needs. We define the capacity and scope before the first month starts.
