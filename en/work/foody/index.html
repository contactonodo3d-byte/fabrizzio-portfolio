Trade show booth / Built project

Foody · ExpoVet

From concept to a 3 × 3 m exhibition stand: brand graphics, a product display, a prize wheel and a flyer with a QR code to the product catalog.

Brief

Foody needed a 3 × 3 m stand for ExpoVet that could carry the brand, display product, and give the team a way to engage visitors. The design had to include brand graphics, a product display, a prize wheel, and a flyer with a QR code that opened the product catalog.

My role

The public case presents the project from concept through to the stand at the fair, including brand graphics, the product display, the prize wheel and the flyer.

Process

I first designed and visualized the 3 × 3 m stand: the overall concept, the layout, the product display, the side graphics, the flyer with the QR code, and the prize wheel as an activation. Those pieces are published as concept renders and visual proposals.

The stand was then built for the fair. The case study shows the realized stand in a general view, the product display from the front and the side, the printed flyer with the QR code, and the prize wheel in use with visitors.

A 3D / AR model of the product display is available for review, separate from the photographs of the built exhibitor.

Explore the product display in 3D / AR

Result

Foody’s 3 × 3 m stand was realized at ExpoVet. A visitor walking the case study can see the concept and the built result for the same elements: the stand, the product display, the flyer and the prize wheel.

Together with El Delta, this is the other project that gives a US buyer what they look for: a render next to a photograph of the finished booth.

Deliverables

  • Concept and layout for a 3 × 3 m stand
  • Brand graphics, including side graphics
  • Product display design, plus a 3D / AR model of the exhibitor
  • Prize wheel activation design
  • Flyer with a QR code to the product catalog
