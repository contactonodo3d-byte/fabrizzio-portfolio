Trade show booth / Built project

Indufar · El Delta

Design and visualization for a built brand space with a nutrition bar, a photo opportunity and an activation — shown as 3D renders next to photographs of the realized space.

Brief

Indufar needed a brand space for El Delta that could do three things at once: serve as a nutrition bar, give visitors a photo opportunity, and hold a live activation. The job was to design that space and visualize it clearly enough to be reviewed, produced and used on the show floor.

My role

I did the design and the 3D visualization. The work was carried out through Péscalo. The project was built.

Process

I designed the TG space for El Delta, including the nutrition bar, the photo opportunity and the activation area, and I visualized the design in 3D so the team could review it before production. The published set is built so a reader can compare the render of the TG space with photographs of the same space during the event.

Result

El Delta was realized. The published photographs show the built space, the photo opportunity and the activation in use during the event, alongside the original design render.

This is the strongest booth project in the portfolio for a US exhibit builder, because it is the one that places a 3D render next to photographs of the finished stand.

Deliverables

The public case study currently includes the TG space render and three event photographs.
