Trade show booths / Five stands for six brands

Indufar Run

Spatial design for TG, Banes, Mentovick, Vitamina C, Arsetonic and Indufar — a family of booths for tasting, product display and interaction.

Brief

Indufar needed a set of booths, not a single stand: five spaces for six brands, each with its own identity, working together as a family. The brands were TG, Banes, Mentovick, Vitamina C, Arsetonic and Indufar. The spaces had to support tasting, product display, interaction and recovery, rather than repeating one layout with a logo swap.

My role

I designed the spaces for TG, Banes, Mentovick, Vitamina C, Arsetonic and Indufar.

Process

The published work presents six brand identities across five booths, including a shared booth for Vitamina C and Arsetonic. Each space has a distinct role:

  • TG — nutrition bar and photo opportunity
  • Banes — Charm Bar and photo opportunity
  • Mentovick — product display and recovery
  • Vitamina C + Arsetonic — shared booth
  • Indufar — history, products and an interactive screen

The images are selected renders from the Indufar Run presentation. They show the design concepts.

Result

The work shows range across several brands in one program: five booths, six identities, and a mix of tasting, display, interaction and recovery. That is useful proof for a builder or an agency that needs more than one space on the same floor.

The public case is still render-led. Unlike El Delta, this set does not yet place built photographs next to the concepts.
