Booth concept · 3D / AR

Gestecner · Navegestic

Two booth concepts for Boat Port Show 2026, built around an aerial drone, an aquatic drone and an isometric map that continues from the wall to the floor.

Brief

Gestecner needed a booth concept for Boat Port Show 2026. The design had to present an aerial drone and an aquatic drone, and use an isometric map as a continuous graphic from the wall to the floor. The approved concept fits a 3 × 3 m area, 3.20 m high, and had to be reviewable in technical views and in 3D, including augmented reality on a compatible phone.

My role

I designed the stand and did the 3D visualization. The work was carried out through Péscalo. The original 3D files are NODO3D. The proposal was not built.

Process

I developed two proposals. Option 1 is the approved concept. It includes an overview with dimensions, a drone display with the isometric map, an interactive screen for mini-games, and a reception area with cartographic graphics. Option 2 is an alternative concept with a different organization of the space and its visual elements, documented with an overview, a floor plan and a top view of the structure.

I also built an interactive 3D model of the concept (7.3 MB, loaded on demand) so a reviewer can rotate the space, take approximate measurements, and, on a compatible phone over HTTPS, open it in augmented reality.

Explore the Gestecner booth in 3D / AR

Result

The client-facing output is a complete concept package: two options, dimensioned technical views, and a 3D/AR model of a 3 × 3 m, 3.20 m-high booth. For a builder or an agency, this study shows how I document a design before anyone produces it.

It is a concept, not a built booth. That should stay on the page so a buyer does not read the renders as construction photography.

Deliverables

  • Two concept proposals (Option 1 approved; Option 2 as an alternative)
  • Design renders and technical views with dimensions, including floor plan and structure views for Option 2
  • Interactive 3D / AR viewer
  • Original 3D files (NODO3D)
