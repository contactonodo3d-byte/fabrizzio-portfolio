How I work

  1. Event and scope

    We review the date, dimensions, budget and fabricator requirements.

  2. Design and review

    I develop the proposal and refine it with your team.

  3. Production handoff

    I prepare the agreed deliverables and coordinate design details with the stand builder.

Before we begin

Can I ask about a project if I already have someone to build the booth?

Yes. Tell me who will produce the space and what documentation they need. We define the design and the files to prepare for that process.

What do I receive at the end?

Deliverables are agreed in the proposal. Depending on the project, they can include renders, a concept presentation, measured views and graphic files for production. We confirm formats and scope before starting.

Does the service include fabrication and installation?

This page presents my design and visualization work. When you ask for a quote, say if you also need production or installation so we can define which part needs a specialist supplier.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the dimensions, complexity, deliverables and the event date. Share those details to receive a proposal adapted to your project.
